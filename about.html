Our story

Where craft meets a growth mindset.

In 2024, our founder Sri Ganesh walked away from a career as a multimedia professional to build something better. The reason was simple: India is full of incredible talent, but most businesses still think design is decoration, not a fire-to-have, not a growth lever.

He'd seen it from the inside. And he knew design can lift about making things look good; it's the engine that drives a business to scale. So he built a studio to prove it.

That belief still shapes our business. Growing up around his family's business, Sri watched his father navigate what it really takes to build something that lasts. This is the DNA behind NeoPixelz: a studio built to help Indian brands compete globally through strong design and precision marketing.

Design isn't about making things look good. It's the engine that drives your business to scale.

In 2024, I left my career as a multimedia professional to become an entrepreneur. India is a powerhouse of talent, but the strategic value of design is still massively undervalued.

Having seen the industry from the inside, I knew design isn't just about looking good, it's about creating real business growth. As a multimedia graduate, I felt a responsibility to raise the bar for how brands communicate.

And that's personal. Growing up in a family business, I watched my father navigate the realities of entrepreneurship. His legacy pushed me to build something that changes how Indian businesses compete worldwide.
